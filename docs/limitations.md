# Limitations

- Contextual extent is not the official legal Broadway Plan boundary.
- Renter exposure, Census DA tenure, confirmed rental units and the four-category exposure matrix are unavailable.
- TRPP context is not an eligibility determination or legal advice.
- Issued permits omit unissued applications and are not construction/completion records.
- Automated permit-to-parcel matches require manual case verification.
- Property assessments are administrative values; land intensity is not rent gap or redevelopment probability.
- Current zoning cannot measure historical zoning uplift.
- Station buffers are straight-line distances; no future travel-time benefits are estimated.
- Descriptive overlap cannot identify causal impacts.
